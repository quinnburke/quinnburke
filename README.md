I am an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Computer Science](https://cs.arizona.edu/) at the [University of Arizona](https://www.arizona.edu/). I completed my PhD in the Computer Sciences Department at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://www.cs.wisc.edu/) in May 2026, where I was a research assistant in the [Security & Privacy Research Group](https://madsp.cs.wisc.edu/) and advised by [Prof. Patrick McDaniel](http://patrickmcdaniel.org).
